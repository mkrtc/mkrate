# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

We expect all participants to:

- Be respectful and considerate
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers:

- For a **private, non-security** report, open a private vulnerability-style
  advisory at <https://github.com/mkrtc/mkrate/security/advisories/new> (it is not
  limited to security issues and stays private to the maintainers), or
- contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile at
  [github.com/mkrtc/mkrate](https://github.com/mkrtc/mkrate).

For general, non-sensitive matters you may also open a
[GitHub Discussion or issue](https://github.com/mkrtc/mkrate/issues).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
